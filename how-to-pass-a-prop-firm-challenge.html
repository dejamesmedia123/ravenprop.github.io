How to Pass a Prop Firm Challenge

A practical, honest plan for passing your challenge: size positions small, respect the limits and stop forcing trades.

2 min read·Updated 30 September 2026
On this page
  1. 1. Know the numbers
  2. 2. Risk a small, fixed amount
  3. 3. Set a daily stop
  4. 4. Do not rush
  5. 5. Keep a journal

Most traders fail because they break a loss limit, not because they cannot find profit. Passing is mostly about controlling risk.

1. Know the numbers

Write down your profit target, daily loss and maximum loss before your first trade.

2. Risk a small, fixed amount

Keep each trade to a small fraction of the account. See how much to risk per trade.

3. Set a daily stop

Stop trading for the day well before the daily limit. A bad day should never end the challenge.

4. Do not rush

A profit target has no need to be hit fast. Fewer, better trades beat many rushed ones.

5. Keep a journal

Record why you entered each trade. Patterns in your mistakes show up quickly.

No method removes risk. You can still lose the challenge fee.
