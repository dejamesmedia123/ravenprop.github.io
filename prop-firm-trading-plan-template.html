Prop Firm Trading Plan Template

A simple written plan to keep you inside the rules during a challenge.

2 min read·Updated 30 September 2026

Write this down before your first trade and keep it beside your chart.

Limits

Daily loss stop (your own, below the firm's), maximum trades per day, risk per trade.

Setup

What you trade, on which timeframe, and the exact entry and exit conditions.

Schedule

The hours you trade and the hours you do not.

After a loss

Stop after a set number of losses and review before the next trade.

Review

Weekly journal check: what worked, what broke the plan.
