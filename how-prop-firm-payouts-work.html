How Prop Firm Payouts Work

Eligibility, requests, review and payment: what happens between profit and money in your bank.

2 min read·Updated 30 September 2026
On this page
  1. Eligibility
  2. Review
  3. Caps and minimums
  4. Proof

Payouts are requested from a funded account once you meet the firm's conditions.

Eligibility

Typical conditions include a minimum profit, a minimum number of trading days and no rule breaches. Your dashboard shows the amount you can request.

Review

Requests are checked against the rules before approval. At Raven Prop, payouts are reviewed then paid to a bank account in your own name.

Caps and minimums

Some plans limit the maximum payout and set a minimum, both as a percentage of account size. Read them before you buy.

Proof

Only trust firms that publish real, verifiable payouts. See our payout proof page.
