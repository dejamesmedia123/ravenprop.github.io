What Is a Prop Trading Firm? A Beginner's Guide

A prop trading firm funds traders who prove their skill, in return for a share of the profits. Here is how it works, what it costs and what to watch for.

2 min read·Updated 30 September 2026
On this page
  1. How it works
  2. What to check first
  3. Risk

A prop trading firm (proprietary trading firm) gives traders access to an account sized by the firm, in return for a share of the profits. Before you get that access, you usually pass an evaluation called a challenge.

How it works

You pay a one-time fee, trade a demo account under fixed rules, and reach a profit target without breaking the loss limits. Pass, and you move to a funded stage where you can request payouts.

What to check first

Read the profit target, daily loss limit, maximum loss limit, profit split and payout rules before you pay. A trustworthy firm shows all of these up front.

Risk

Your main cost is the challenge fee, which you can lose if you fail. Only pay what you can afford to lose. The account size is not a deposit, and rewards depend on results and are never guaranteed.
