10 Risk Management Rules for Prop Traders

Ten simple rules that protect your challenge from the loss limits.

2 min read·Updated 30 September 2026
On this page
  1. Rules
  2. Related

Good risk management is the difference between passing and resetting.

Rules

  1. Risk a fixed small percentage per trade.
  2. Always use a stop loss.
  3. Set a daily loss stop below the firm's limit.
  4. Limit open trades at once.
  5. Avoid trading through major news if you cannot manage the spread.
  6. Never add to a losing trade.
  7. Do not increase size after a loss.
  8. Take a break after two or three losses.
  9. Check the rules on swaps, weekends and lot sizes.
  10. Keep a trading journal and review it weekly.
