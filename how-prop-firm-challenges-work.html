How Do Prop Firm Challenges Work?

Phases, profit targets, loss limits and minimum days explained in plain language, with a worked example.

2 min read·Updated 30 September 2026
On this page
  1. Phases
  2. Profit target
  3. Loss limits
  4. After you pass

A prop firm challenge is a rule-based test on a demo account. You must hit a profit target while staying inside loss limits.

Phases

Some challenges have two phases, each with its own profit target. Others have one phase. Raven Prop lists the number of phases on every plan.

Profit target

The percentage gain you must reach. On a $10,000 account with a 10% target, that is $1,000 of profit.

Loss limits

The daily loss limit caps how much you can lose in one day. The maximum loss limit caps total losses. Breaking either ends the challenge.

After you pass

You move to a funded stage and can request payouts once you are eligible. See how payouts work.
