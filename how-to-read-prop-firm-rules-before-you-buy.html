How to Read Prop Firm Rules Before You Buy

A pre-purchase checklist so no rule surprises you after you pay.

Spend ten minutes on the rules before you pay. It saves fees.

Checklist

  1. Profit target for each phase.
  2. Daily and maximum loss limits, and whether they are static or trailing.
  3. Minimum trading days and inactivity rules.
  4. Profit split and how it changes.
  5. Payout caps, minimums and review steps.
  6. Restricted trading such as news, weekends or lot sizes.
  7. Fees, including any pass or reset fee.
  8. Refund terms.

Red flag

If a rule is hidden or vague, ask support first. Raven Prop lists targets and loss limits on every plan before you pay.
