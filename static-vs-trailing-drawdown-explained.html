Static vs Trailing Drawdown Explained

How static and trailing loss limits differ, with a numbers example.

The drawdown type decides where your loss line sits as you make profit.

Static

The line is fixed from the start. On $10,000 with a 10% static limit, it stays at $9,000 even if you grow to $11,000.

Trailing

The line rises with your best balance. If you reach $11,000 with a 10% trailing limit, the line moves to $9,900.

Why it matters

Trailing limits leave less room after a winning run. Static limits are generally easier to plan around.
