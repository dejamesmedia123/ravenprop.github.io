Prop Firm Red Flags: How to Spot a Bad Firm

Warning signs to check before you send money to any prop firm.

2 min read·Updated 30 September 2026
On this page

  2. Guaranteed profit
  3. Fake proof
  4. Pay to someone else
  5. No real support

Not every firm is trustworthy. Look for these warning signs.

Hidden rules

Terms that appear only after payment.

Guaranteed profit

No firm can guarantee results. Any promise of guaranteed rewards is a warning.

Fake proof

Payout screenshots with no context. Look for verifiable, dated proof.

Pay to someone else

Payment to a personal account or an unrelated name with no explanation.

No real support

No contact, no ticket system, no company details.

Check the basics

Read the Terms, Privacy and Risk pages, then search for independent reviews and read both good and bad ones.
