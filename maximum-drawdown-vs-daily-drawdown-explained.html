Daily Drawdown vs Maximum Drawdown Explained

Two different loss limits that end challenges. Learn how each is measured and how to stay safe.

2 min read·Updated 30 September 2026
On this page
  1. Daily drawdown
  2. Maximum drawdown
  3. Static vs trailing
  4. Tip

Drawdown is how far your account falls from a reference point. Prop firms use two limits.

Daily drawdown

The most you may lose in a single day. If you start the day at $10,000 with a 5% daily limit, you must not fall below $9,500 that day.

Maximum drawdown

The most you may lose overall. With a 10% static limit on $10,000, the account must never fall below $9,000.

Static vs trailing

A static limit stays fixed from the starting balance. A trailing limit moves up as your balance grows. Raven Prop shows the type on each plan.

Tip

Treat the daily limit as your real limit and stop earlier. Open trades count toward drawdown, not just closed ones.
