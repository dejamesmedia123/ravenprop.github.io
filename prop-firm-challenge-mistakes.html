Prop Firm Challenge Mistakes That Make Traders Fail

The most common reasons challenges fail, and how to avoid each one.

2 min read·Updated 30 September 2026
On this page
  1. Over-risking
  2. Ignoring the daily limit
  3. Revenge trading
  4. Trading with no plan
  5. Skipping the rules
  6. Buying too big too soon

These mistakes appear again and again.

Over-risking

Big positions turn one bad trade into a breached limit.

Ignoring the daily limit

Traders track profit but forget the daily loss line. Set an alert well before it.

Revenge trading

Trying to win back a loss quickly usually makes it bigger.

Trading with no plan

Without entry, stop and target defined in advance, decisions become emotional.

Skipping the rules

Read swap, weekend, news and inactivity rules. Some challenges end after too many days with no trading.

Buying too big too soon

A smaller plan lets you learn the process for a lower fee.
